Scents Shouldn’t Hurt.

A healthier future is fragrance free.

Fragranced products can trigger migraines, breathing problems, allergic-type reactions, nausea, brain fog, asthma symptoms, and other health effects.

Fragrance is not just perfume.
It can be found in laundry detergent, fabric softener, dryer sheets, hand lotion, hair products, deodorant, cleaners, air fresheners, scented soap, and more.

Our Mission

Fragrance Free Future raises awareness about:

  • fragrance exposure
  • fragrance sensitivity
  • accessible indoor air
  • the health and social impacts of fragranced environments

We help people understand how fragranced products and spaces can affect:

  • health
  • accessibility
  • inclusion
  • participation in everyday life

Fragrance Free Future also creates space for:

  • community voices
  • lived experiences
  • public participation
  • greater awareness of invisible barriers

Through stories, visuals, educational materials, and public awareness campaigns, we support healthier, more accessible indoor spaces and broader cultural change.

Why This Matters

Many people experience migraines, asthma symptoms, mast cell reactions, breathing difficulties, nausea, cognitive effects, and other health impacts from fragranced products.

Fragrance exposure happens in workplaces, schools, apartments, transit, healthcare settings, stores, and public spaces every day.
